# 1. Adopt Multi-Cloud Strategy

Date: 2025-06-15

## Status

Completed

## Context

BelFoot FC operates a stadium with latency-critical gameday systems (ticketing, access control, IoT sensors) alongside cloud-native digital fan services and sporting analytics. A single-cloud strategy cannot optimally serve all workload profiles.

## Decision

Adopt a multi-cloud strategy with three deployment zones:

- **On-Premise (Stadium Data Center)** for latency-critical gameday operations (ticketing, stadium management, IoT gateway)
- **Azure Cloud** for digital fan platform, data platform, integration backbone, and corporate systems
- **AWS Cloud** for sporting analytics, player performance, and medical records (leveraging AWS sports analytics ecosystem)

## Consequences

- Increased infrastructure complexity requiring cross-cloud networking and identity federation
- Need for a unified integration platform (Azure Service Bus) to bridge all three zones
- Operational teams must maintain skills across Azure, AWS, and on-premise infrastructure
- Improved resilience through workload distribution and zone-appropriate optimization
